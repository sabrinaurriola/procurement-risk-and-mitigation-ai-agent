# Contingency Sourcing and Alternative Supplier Qualification Policy (FICTIONAL)

## Purpose and status of this document
This is a fictional internal policy of the fictional company Bakery Foods Inc., written for a portfolio simulation. It does not describe any real company or regulation. The roles named in this document, such as the Procurement Director and the Raw Materials Procurement Manager, are fictional organizational assumptions made for the simulation, not descriptions of real positions. The Raw Materials Procurement Manager reviews and recommends; final approval authority rests with the Procurement Director.

## Alternative suppliers are leads until qualified
Companies found through desk research are sourcing leads. During qualification the team may request non-binding samples, carry out technical evaluation and verify the lead's claims. A lead must not receive an order, a contract, a purchasing commitment or a supply-volume commitment until it has been qualified and the appropriate approval has been given. Information published by the company itself is self-reported and must be verified independently. The risk assessment agent may suggest leads, but it cannot qualify or approve a supplier.

## Qualification steps
Qualification covers verification of the legal entity and its location, comparison of the offered product with the required specification, evidence of food-safety certification, laboratory testing of samples, confirmation of capacity and delivery terms, and review of commercial terms. The Procurement Director approves a qualified supplier in writing.

## Crude and refined oil
Crude sunflower oil needs refining before use in food production. It is not interchangeable with refined, food-grade sunflower oil and the two must never be compared as if they were the same product.

## Records
For every lead the team records the source of each claim, the date it was retrieved and the person who verified it.
