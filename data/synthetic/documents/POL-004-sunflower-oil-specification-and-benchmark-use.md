# Sunflower Oil Specification and Benchmark Use (FICTIONAL)

## Purpose and status of this document
This is a fictional internal policy of the fictional company Bakery Foods Inc., written for a portfolio simulation. It does not describe any real regulation.

## Required product
The three factories use refined, food-grade sunflower oil. Supplier offers for crude oil are not equivalent and are assessed separately, including the cost and the capability of refining.

## Use of public benchmarks
Public benchmark prices are market indicators, not supplier quotes. The benchmark used in risk assessments is a crude sunflower oil price, so the premium of refined oil over the benchmark is an assumption that the team reviews each quarter.

## Data quality
Benchmark observations that are flagged as outliers, missing or otherwise invalid are excluded from calculations and kept in the database. A benchmark that is older than the freshness limit set by the procurement team (currently provisional) is not used for exposure estimates.
