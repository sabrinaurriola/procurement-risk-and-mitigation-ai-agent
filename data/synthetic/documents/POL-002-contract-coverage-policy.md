# Contract Coverage and Price-Risk Policy (FICTIONAL)

## Purpose and status of this document
This is a fictional internal policy of the fictional company Bakery Foods Inc., written for a portfolio simulation. It is internal context only and does not describe any real regulation. The roles named in this document, such as the Procurement Director and the Raw Materials Procurement Manager, are fictional organizational assumptions made for the simulation, not descriptions of real positions. The Raw Materials Procurement Manager reviews and recommends; final approval authority rests with the Procurement Director.

## Coverage objective
Procurement aims to cover a majority of the forecast demand of each key ingredient for the coming three months with fixed-price or index-linked contracts. Coverage below this objective in any month must be reported to the Raw Materials Procurement Manager together with proposed actions.

## Contract instruments
Fixed-price contracts protect the company from market movements for the contracted volume while they are valid. Index-linked contracts follow a public benchmark through an agreed pass-through factor. Spot purchases are permitted for the residual volume, but they should not be the main source of supply for a key ingredient.

## Escalation of growing uncovered volume
When uncovered volume increases from one month to the next, for example because a contract ends during the horizon, the Raw Materials Procurement Manager performs a coverage review and recommends whether to extend, replace or add contracts. The review is recorded and any decision remains with the people who hold approval authority; contract commitments are approved by the Procurement Director.

## Limits of scenario analysis
Scenario results are indicative stress tests under stated assumptions. They are not budgets, forecasts or supplier quotes, and they must not be used to commit spending.
