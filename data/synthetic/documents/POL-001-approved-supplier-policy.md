# Approved Supplier Policy (FICTIONAL)

## Purpose and status of this document
This is a fictional internal policy of the fictional company Bakery Foods Inc., written for a portfolio simulation. It does not describe any real company, law or regulation, and it is not evidence of regulatory compliance. The roles named in this document, such as the Procurement Director, Legal and the Raw Materials Procurement Manager, are fictional organizational assumptions made for the simulation, not descriptions of real positions. The Raw Materials Procurement Manager reviews and recommends; final approval authority rests with the Procurement Director and, for blocked suppliers, with Legal.

## Approval statuses
Every supplier carries one approval status. An approved supplier may hold new contracts and renewals. A conditional supplier may deliver under existing contracts while the listed conditions, for example a pending audit, remain open. An unverified supplier may not receive any commercial commitment until qualification is complete. A blocked supplier receives no new orders and its existing contracts are reviewed by Legal. Only people with approval authority can change a status; decision-support tools, including the risk assessment agent, can recommend but never approve.

## Conditional suppliers
A conditional supplier may continue to deliver under its existing valid contract, subject to internal monitoring, while its listed conditions remain open. The outstanding conditions must be completed and verified. Renewal of a contract with a conditional supplier, or an increase in its volume, requires sign-off by the Procurement Director in writing and cannot be committed until all open conditions are closed. Nothing in this policy permits a renewal or volume increase while any condition remains open. The Raw Materials Procurement Manager may initiate a review, coordinate corrective actions with the supplier and recommend escalation to the Procurement Director, but cannot approve a renewal or increase. Decision-support tools, including the risk assessment agent, cannot approve suppliers or authorize contractual changes. The procurement team should avoid placing a large share of an ingredient's committed volume with conditionally approved suppliers. Any material share must be reported in the monthly risk review together with the planned closing date of the open conditions.

## Review triggers
A supplier status is reviewed when an audit date passes without a result, when a certificate expires, or when a serious quality or delivery incident is reported. The outcome of each review is recorded with the date and the reviewer.
